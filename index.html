the name

एक साथ

/ek saath/ — together.

two words. one promise. a corner of bhilai where strangers become regulars, where a slow afternoon is the whole point, and where the cup in front of you is reason enough to stay.

built around raw concrete, warm light, and a wall of green — so the city falls quiet the moment you sit down.
